# H1 — Madaba: A Mosaic Chapter of Jordan

Madaba, south of Amman, is one of Jordan's best-known historic towns and an easy addition to routes toward Mount Nebo, the Dead Sea and Petra. The city is particularly associated with its mosaic heritage.

## Why Visit Madaba?
Madaba gives travelers a different experience from Jordan's monumental desert and archaeological landscapes. Its historic churches, archaeological remains and mosaics make it a useful cultural stop on a wider Jordan itinerary.

## Madaba and St. George's Church
One of Madaba's most recognized attractions is St. George's Church, associated with the famous mosaic map of the Holy Land. Religious and archaeological details should be presented with accurate attribution in final editorial content.
