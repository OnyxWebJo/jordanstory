# H1 — Aqaba: Your Jordan Story Reaches the Red Sea

Aqaba is Jordan's coastal city on the Red Sea and brings a completely different atmosphere to a journey through the country. After archaeological sites and desert landscapes, travelers can add sea views, relaxation and marine activities.

## Why Visit Aqaba?
Aqaba can offer:
- Red Sea coastline
- beach time
- snorkeling and diving opportunities
- resorts and relaxation
- access to southern Jordan routes

Activities such as diving and snorkeling must never be described as included unless the selected package explicitly includes them.

## Aqaba and Wadi Rum
Aqaba and Wadi Rum are both in southern Jordan and can be combined within a wider itinerary.

## Aqaba, Wadi Rum and Petra
Travelers with several days can create a southern Jordan route linking Petra, Wadi Rum and Aqaba.

## Aqaba from Amman
