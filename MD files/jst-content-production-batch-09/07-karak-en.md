# H1 — Karak Castle: A Fortress on the Road South

Karak Castle stands above the city of Al-Karak in southern-central Jordan. Its strategic position and medieval history make it a meaningful stop on some routes between Amman and Petra.

## Why Visit Karak Castle?
Karak adds a fortress experience to itineraries otherwise dominated by ancient cities, religious sites and desert landscapes.

## Karak on the Way to Petra
Several Jordan itineraries travel south from Amman toward Petra. Where time and routing allow, Karak can become a historical stop along that journey.

Jordan Story Tours' legacy multi-day itineraries include Karak in selected programs.

## FAQ
### Where is Karak Castle?
It is in the city of Al-Karak, south of Amman.

### Can Karak be combined with Petra?
Yes. It can fit into some southbound itineraries toward Petra.

### Is Karak a day trip from Amman?
It is possible, but many travelers encounter Karak as part of a longer route south.
