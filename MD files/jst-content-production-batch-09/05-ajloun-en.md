# H1 — Ajloun Castle: A Northern Chapter Above the Hills

Ajloun Castle rises above the green hills of northern Jordan. The medieval fortress adds a very different historical landscape to a journey that may also include the Roman-era city of Jerash.

## Why Visit Ajloun Castle?
The castle offers:
- medieval military architecture
- elevated views
- historical context from the Islamic medieval period
- an easy combination with Jerash

## FAQ
### Where is Ajloun Castle?
Ajloun Castle is in northern Jordan, in the hills around Ajloun.

### Can I visit Ajloun from Amman?
Yes. It can be visited on a day trip from Amman.

### Should I combine Ajloun with Jerash?
They are commonly combined and provide contrasting archaeological and historical experiences.
