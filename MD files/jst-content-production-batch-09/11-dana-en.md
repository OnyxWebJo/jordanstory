# H1 — Dana: Jordan's Story Turns Toward Nature

Dana offers a quieter chapter of Jordan: a historic village setting, dramatic terrain and access to one of the country's important nature-conservation landscapes.

Dana appeals particularly to travelers interested in:

## FAQ
### Where is Dana?
Dana is in southern-central Jordan, on routes that can connect toward Petra.

### Is Dana only for hikers?
No. Hiking is a major reason to visit, but travelers can also appreciate the village, viewpoints and landscape.

### Can Dana be combined with Petra?
Yes. Dana can be incorporated into longer southbound itineraries.

### How long should I spend in Dana?
That depends on whether you are stopping for scenery or planning a hike or overnight stay.
