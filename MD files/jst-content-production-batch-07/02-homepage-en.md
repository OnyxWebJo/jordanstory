# Frequently Asked Questions

## How many days do I need to visit Jordan?
A short trip can cover highlights such as Petra, while five to seven days gives travelers more time to combine Petra, Wadi Rum, the Dead Sea, Amman and other destinations. The right duration depends on arrival times, pace and the places you want to include.

## Can I visit Petra from Amman in one day?
Yes. Jordan Story Tours currently offers a one-day Petra tour from Amman. Because the road journey is substantial, an early departure gives you more time at Petra.

## Can I combine Petra and Wadi Rum?
Yes. Petra and Wadi Rum are commonly combined because both are in southern Jordan. They can be part of a long day trip or, more comfortably, a multi-day itinerary.

## Can Jordan Story Tours create a private itinerary?
Yes. Use the custom-tour request to share your dates, group size and preferred destinations.

## Do you provide airport transportation?
Jordan Story Tours currently publishes airport and land-transfer services using different vehicle categories. Final prices should be confirmed for your route and group size.
