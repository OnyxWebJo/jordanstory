# H1 — Petra, Jordan: Walk Into the Story

Petra is an ancient Nabataean city in southern Jordan, best known for its rock-cut architecture and the dramatic approach through the Siq. For many travelers, the first view of Al-Khazneh — the Treasury — is the defining moment of a trip to Jordan.

## The Journey Through the Siq
The experience begins before the Treasury appears. The Siq is the narrow natural passage leading into Petra, with sandstone walls rising on both sides. This approach is central to the way visitors experience the site — and it inspires the opening sequence of the new Jordan Story experience.

## What to See in Petra
Important areas commonly visited include:
- the Siq
- Al-Khazneh / the Treasury
- Street of Facades
- Royal Tombs
- Colonnaded Street
- the Monastery, for travelers with enough time and mobility

The exact route depends on available time, walking ability and site conditions.

## How Much Time Do You Need?
A one-day visit can cover Petra's principal route and several major monuments. Travelers who want a slower pace, longer hikes or more extensive exploration may prefer additional time.

## FAQ

### Where is Petra?
Petra is in southern Jordan, near the modern town of Wadi Musa.

### Can I visit Petra in one day?
Yes. A day is enough to experience the main route and major landmarks, although longer stays allow more extensive exploration.

### Is Petra suitable for a day trip from Amman?
Yes, but it is a long day because of the driving distance. An early departure is recommended.

### Can I combine Petra with Wadi Rum?
Yes. The two destinations are commonly combined in southern Jordan itineraries.
