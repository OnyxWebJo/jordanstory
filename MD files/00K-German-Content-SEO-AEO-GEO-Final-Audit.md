**Priority:** CRITICAL
**Run after:** 00I + 00J
**Fact source:** PARITY_VERIFIED master tour data only.

## Mission
Finalize the German public experience as a native German travel website, not a literal translation of English.

## Search-language direction
Research and assign page ownership around natural German travel terminology such as:
- Jordanien Rundreise
- Jordanien Privatreise
- individuelle Jordanien Rundreise
- Jordanien Reise
- Jordanien Reiseveranstalter
- Petra Jordanien
- Petra Tour ab Amman
- Wadi Rum
- Totes Meer Jordanien
- Jordanien Rundreise 7 Tage / 8 Tage
- deutschsprachige Reiseleitung Jordanien
- Jordanien Kulturreise
- Jordanien Luxusreise

These are research seeds, not automatic final keywords.

## Locked product
Never change itinerary, duration, route, meals, accommodation, Included/Excluded, entrance fees, transfers, driver/guide conditions or price logic during German localization.

## Required German page fields
For every indexable page:
- localized URL/slug
- SEO title
- meta description
- H1
- introduction
- body/editorial content
- localized itinerary narrative without factual changes
- Inklusive / Nicht inklusive
- meals
- FAQ/AEO
- CTA
- image alt text where meaningful
- internal links
- schema strings where applicable

## Native terminology
Prefer natural German travel vocabulary according to intent:
`Rundreise`, `Privatreise`, `individuelle Reise`, `Reiseverlauf`, `Reiseleitung`, `Fahrer`, `Unterkunft`, `Mahlzeiten`, `Inklusive Leistungen`, `Nicht inklusive`, `Totes Meer`.

Do not mix English UI labels into German pages.

## Driver vs Guide
Translate the verified service accurately. `englischsprachiger Fahrer` must never become `deutschsprachiger Reiseleiter` unless the product actually includes one.

## Meals
Render from shared structured meal data. Never infer Halbpension/Vollpension.

## Keyword map
Create `docs/SEO-KEYWORD-MAP-DE.md` with:

## AEO
Create `docs/AEO-QUESTION-MAP-DE.md`. Questions must sound native and cover actual German traveler concerns, including itinerary, meals, guide, entrance fees, Petra, Wadi Rum, Totes Meer, private tours and booking.

## GEO
Create `docs/GEO-ENTITY-MAP-DE.md`. Entity facts remain identical to English; descriptions are localized.

## Story
Preserve Jordan Story emotion without forcing literal translations of “Story” into every headline. The German copy should sound editorial and premium.

## QA
No English leftovers. No translated English keywords without German SERP research. No commercial-fact divergence. No duplicate titles/metas. No fake ratings.
