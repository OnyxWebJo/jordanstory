## Reiseverlauf
### Day 1 — Queen Alia Airport – Amman
Airport arrival, representative/visa assistance, transfer to Amman hotel.

### Day 2 — Amman – Madaba – Mount Nebo – Dead Sea – Overnight in Petra
Breakfast; Madaba/St George; Mount Nebo; Dead Sea relaxation; drive to Petra for overnight.

### Day 3 — Petra Visit – Overnight in Wadi Rum
Breakfast in Petra; approximately four hours exploring Petra; drive south to Wadi Rum for overnight.

### Day 4 — Wadi Rum Tour – Amman Airport
Breakfast in Wadi Rum; return drive to Amman airport for departure.
