# H1 — Jerash: Walk Through Jordan's Roman-Era City

Jerash, north of Amman, preserves an extensive archaeological city with monumental streets, plazas, temples and theaters. Its location makes it one of the easiest major historical destinations to combine with Amman.

## What to See in Jerash
Visitors commonly encounter:
- Hadrian's Arch
- the Hippodrome area
- Oval Plaza
- the Cardo
- temples
- theaters
- the Nymphaeum
- churches and later-period remains

## Jerash from Amman
Jerash is well suited to a day trip from Amman.

Jordan Story Tours currently offers routes combining:
- Jerash & Ajloun
- Jerash, Ajloun & Umm Qais

## Why Combine Jerash and Ajloun?
The two destinations are both in northern Jordan but offer different experiences: Jerash focuses on a large archaeological city, while Ajloun adds a hilltop medieval castle and northern landscape.
