# H1 — Amman: Where Your Jordan Story Meets the Present

Amman is Jordan's capital and the starting point for many journeys across the country. Ancient landmarks sit above busy streets, local markets, restaurants and modern neighborhoods.

## What to See in Amman
### Amman Citadel
The Citadel occupies a prominent hill above central Amman and contains archaeological remains from several historical periods.

### Roman Theater
The Roman Theater is one of downtown Amman's most recognizable ancient landmarks.

### Downtown Amman
Downtown brings together markets, restaurants, shops, historic streets and everyday city life.

## Amman as Your Base for Jordan
Many of Jordan's major destinations can be reached from Amman through day tours or as the first stage of a longer itinerary.

Popular routes include:
- Amman → Jerash & Ajloun
- Amman → Madaba & Mount Nebo
- Amman → Dead Sea
- Amman → Petra
- Amman → Desert Castles

## Airport Arrival
Queen Alia International Airport is the principal international gateway for Amman and many Jordan itineraries. Jordan Story Tours currently publishes airport-transfer services with several vehicle categories.

## FAQ
### Is Amman worth visiting?
Yes. Amman offers important archaeological sites as well as a useful introduction to contemporary Jordanian city life.

### How long should I spend in Amman?
Many travelers can see key central landmarks in a day, while additional time allows a slower city experience and nearby excursions.

### Can I take day tours from Amman?
Yes. Amman is the departure point for many Jordan Story Tours day trips.

### Is Petra possible as a day trip from Amman?
Yes, although the journey makes it a long day and an early departure is advisable.
