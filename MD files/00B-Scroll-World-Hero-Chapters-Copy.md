## 1. Critical Rule — Scenes Are Not Chapters

Technical animation states such as `Siq Entrance`, `Deeper Siq`, `Canyon Bend`, `Treasury First Glimpse`, `Treasury Partial Reveal`, and `Desert Transition` are INTERNAL ONLY.

Never show technical labels such as:
- SCENE 01 / SCENE 02
- CANYON CLOSES
- THE BEND
- TREASURY PARTIAL REVEAL
- DESERT TRANSITION
- asset filenames or debug names

The visitor-facing narrative contains only:

`OPENING → CHAPTER 01 PETRA → PETRA REVEAL → TRANSITION → CHAPTER 02 WADI RUM → CHOOSE YOUR JORDAN STORY`

The visual animation can contain many scenes inside one chapter.

# 17. SEO / AEO / GEO Protection

The Scroll World is a visual storytelling layer, not a replacement for crawlable content.

Maintain real semantic HTML for Jordan Story Tours, Jordan tours, private tours, Petra, Wadi Rum, Amman, Dead Sea, Jerash, tour categories, custom tours, booking, internal links, FAQs and organization information.

Essential information must never exist only inside WebGL, canvas, video, images or animation timelines.

After the hero, use direct-answer content for genuine questions such as:
- Can you visit Petra and Wadi Rum on the same Jordan tour?
- How many days do you need to visit Jordan?
- Can Jordan Story Tours arrange a private tour?

Make entity relationships explicit: Jordan Story Tours offers tours; tours visit destinations; destinations connect to relevant tours and guides.

Separate internal animation metadata from public content.

```ts

  id: "treasury_partial_reveal",
  debugName: "Treasury Partial Reveal", // INTERNAL ONLY
  publicContent: null

```

For Petra:

```ts
{
  id: "petra_full_reveal",
  debugName: "Treasury Full Reveal",
  publicContent: {
    chapter: "CHAPTER 01 · PETRA",
    title: "Petra",
    subtitle: "The Rose City, revealed.",
    description: "Walk through the Siq and stand before the Treasury — a moment thousands of years in the making.",
    cta: "Explore Petra"
  }
}
```

Never render `debugName`.

# 20. Final QA

Reject the homepage if the visitor sees any technical scene terminology.

The visitor should perceive only:

`Your Jordan Story → Chapter 01 Petra → journey through the Siq → Petra revealed → story continues → Chapter 02 Wadi Rum → Choose Your Jordan Story`

Do not narrate every camera movement. The visual world communicates movement; the copy communicates **Story**.

Emotional rhythm:

**Curiosity → Journey → Anticipation → Discovery → Wonder → Transition → Freedom → Choice**

The visitor should finish the hero thinking:

**I want to experience Jordan.**

The website should then answer:

**Here is the story you can book.**
