## Existing Tour Details
If your arrival is early, this package is presented as an affordable Jordan itinerary covering Petra, Dead Sea, Amman and Jerash.

## Existing Itinerary
### Day 1 — QUEEN ALIA AIRPORT – PETRA VISIT
Airport representative/visa assistance; drive about 3 hours to Petra; Petra visit.

### Day 2 — PETRA – DEAD SEA – AMMAN
Breakfast in Petra; drive to Dead Sea; resort/relaxation/floating; lunch; continue to Amman for overnight.

### Day 3 — AMMAN CITY TOUR – JERASH – AMMAN AIRPORT
Breakfast in Amman; Amman city/Citadel/King Abdullah Mosque/downtown; Jerash; return to Amman airport.
