# Tour Category Landing Pages — EN + DE Specification

## Required Category Pages
1. Day Tours / Tagesausflüge
2. Budget Tours / Preisbewusste Rundreisen
3. Classical Tours / Klassische Jordanien-Rundreisen
4. Luxury Tours / Luxusreisen
5. Biblical & Holy Land Tours / Biblische & Heilig-Land-Reisen
6. Islamic & Historical Tours / Islamische & historische Reisen

## Category Page SEO Pattern
Each category page needs:
- unique search-intent title/H1
- 100–180 word answer-first introduction
- filterable tour cards
- destination entity links
- “who this is for” section
- FAQ
- custom-tour CTA
- breadcrumb
- EN/DE reciprocal hreflang

## Do Not
- duplicate the same paragraph across categories
- stuff “Jordan tours” repeatedly
- hide actual tour names behind Story-only language
- publish a tour card whose availability is `hidden`

## Story Layer
Use Story as an experience system:
- Choose Your Story
- Short Stories: Day Tours
- Classic Jordan Story
- Sacred Stories
- Desert & History Chapters

German must use natural equivalents rather than forced literal translations.
