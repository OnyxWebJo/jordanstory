# Batch 06 — Jordan Story Brand Content Evidence

The current website already uses Story as part of the product language.

Confirmed legacy concepts include:
- Our Story
- About Jordan Story
- Your Journey Starts Here
- Begin your Story
- “Start your story in Jordan” inside tour copy

## Migration Direction
Preserve the brand equity but improve the execution.

### Product-language system
Possible experience labels:
- Start Your Story
- Choose Your Story
- Your Jordan Story
- Story Route
- Story Chapters
- Continue the Story
- Build Your Story

### Immersive homepage
The Siq → Treasury → Wadi Rum scroll experience should function as the opening **chapter** of the visitor's Jordan Story, not as a disconnected 3D demo.

### Tour pages
Tours should remain searchable by practical intent (duration, destination, type, price), while Story language becomes the emotional/product layer around that utility.

### SEO rule
Do not replace high-intent search language such as “Petra tour from Amman” with vague brand-only titles. Story branding complements SEO; it does not hide what the page sells.
