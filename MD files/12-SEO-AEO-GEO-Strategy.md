# 12 — SEO, AEO & GEO Strategy
## Jordan Story Tours — English + German

**Purpose:** Define the organic-search and AI-discovery system for the redesigned Jordan Story Tours website.

**Primary objective:** Build a website that can rank for commercially valuable Jordan travel searches while becoming a clear, trustworthy source that search engines and AI answer systems can understand and cite.

**Languages:** English + German

---

# 1. Strategic Principle

Do not optimize only for keywords.

Build a connected knowledge and commercial ecosystem:

**Jordan → Destinations → Tours → Story Collections → Travel Guides → FAQs → Booking**

Every page must have a clear search intent and business purpose.

---

# 2. Three Optimization Layers

## SEO — Search Engine Optimization
Goal:
- rankings
- impressions
- clicks
- qualified organic traffic
- bookings

## AEO — Answer Engine Optimization
Goal:
- answer traveler questions directly
- win useful answer surfaces
- make content easy to extract accurately

## GEO — Generative Engine Optimization
Goal:
- make Jordan Story Tours and its content understandable to AI systems
- strengthen entity relationships
- publish clear, useful, evidence-based information worthy of citation

These are one content architecture, not three separate websites.

# 17. Answer-First Writing

For question sections:

**Answer first → Context second → Detail third**

Example:

### How many days do you need in Petra?

Start with a concise answer based on realistic traveler scenarios, then explain one-day versus longer visits.

Do not begin with 300 words of history before answering the question.

Avoid vague statements.

“Petra is not far from Amman.”

# 20. GEO Content Principle

AI systems should be able to identify explicit subject-predicate relationships.

Example:

**Jordan Story Tours offers a private Petra and Wadi Rum tour departing from Amman.**

**Discover the magic of two unforgettable worlds with us.**

Use the factual sentence first; emotional storytelling can follow.

# 37. 3D Hero SEO Safeguard

The immersive Siq → Treasury → Wadi Rum experience is enhancement.

Implementation pattern:

1. server-render semantic homepage shell
2. load optimized fallback/poster
3. initialize immersive experience progressively
4. retain real HTML headings/copy
5. keep destination/tour links crawlable
6. support reduced motion
7. provide low-power/mobile fallback

Do not hide the homepage inside a canvas.
