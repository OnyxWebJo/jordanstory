Eastern Desert Castles (Qusayr Amra, Kharana & Azraq)

Umayyad Caliphate Hunting Lodges, Fortresses & Fresco Palaces

Overview & Landmark Significance

Scattered across Jordan's eastern basalt desert, the Umayyad Desert Castles (7th-8th century AD) served as royal hunting retreats, caravan stops, and meeting halls. Highlights include UNESCO Qusayr Amra's wall frescoes and Azraq's basalt fort.

Key Experiences & Highlights

•UNESCO World Heritage Qusayr Amra bathhouse fresco paintings
•Formidable square walls and courtyard of Qasr Kharana
•Black basalt castle at Azraq Oasis used by Lawrence of Arabia

Practical Travel & Insider Tips

Best Time to Visit: October to April
✓Combine Kharana, Amra, and Azraq into a single 4-hour morning circuit from Amman.
✓Look for the Zodiac dome painting inside Qusayr Amra bathhouse.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Qusayr Amra a UNESCO World Heritage site?

Qusayr Amra was designated UNESCO World Heritage in 1985 due to its exceptionally rare 8th-century early Islamic wall frescoes portraying hunting scenes and astronomy.
