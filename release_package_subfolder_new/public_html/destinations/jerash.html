Roman Jerash

Pompeii of the East

Best Season: March to May

About Roman Jerash

One of the best-preserved Roman provincial cities in the world. Walk along the Oval Plaza, Cardo Maximus, and grand temples of Artemis and Zeus.

Key Highlights & Must-See Landmarks

  • Hadrian's Arch gate
  • The Oval Forum plaza
  • South Theater acoustics test
  • Cardo Maximus chariot grooves

Local Insider Tips

  • 1Climb to the top of the South Theater for acoustic bagpipe performance.
  • 2Visit in spring (March/April) when wild green hills surround the marble ruins.
